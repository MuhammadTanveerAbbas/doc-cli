# Code of conduct

We want a respectful, harassment free community for everyone who takes part in this project.

Expected behavior: be kind, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what helps the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior: harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing private information, and any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, and contributions that break these rules and may ban people who repeatedly do so. Report problems privately to the maintainers. Reports are handled with discretion.
